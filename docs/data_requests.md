# Dermo data available only on request (PLAN.md P4.2j)

Public surveillance gives bar-level means once a year. The per-oyster and monthly records behind them are held
by the programs below. Each request is one email; the text to send is at the bottom. Addresses are not listed
here because they were not verified from official pages; use each program's current contact page.

| Holder | Data | Years | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maryland DNR, Cooperative Oxford Laboratory (Fall Oyster Survey disease program) | Per-oyster Dermo intensity scores (0-7 scale) behind the published bar means, 43 Disease Bars | 1990-2024 | Turns 1,087 bar means into about 32,000 oyster-level observations; the only way to fit intensity distributions in Maryland |
| Connecticut Department of Agriculture, Bureau of Aquaculture (Milford) | Station-level Dermo prevalence and intensity (histology 1997-2016, PCR 2019 on) for the ~14 monitoring sites | 1997-2024 | Cool, high-salinity Long Island Sound sites; only pooled coastwide averages are public |
| NOAA Milford Laboratory | Monthly qPCR Dermo series with co-located water quality (Ash Creek, Fence Creek, Huntington Harbor) | about 2015 on | Monthly resolution at cool, salty sites; not public |
| Rutgers Haskin Shellfish Research Laboratory (Delaware Bay oyster disease monitoring; Bushek et al. 2012 data) | Bed-level prevalence and weighted prevalence by year, and the oyster-level scores behind them | 1990-present | Longest Northeast series with intensity; 1990s report tables are scanned and need the data file |
| New Hampshire Fish and Game (Great Bay oyster disease testing) | Reports after 2019, and the laboratory sheets behind the class counts | 2019-present | Extends the only cool, salty intensity series past 2019 |

## Request text (adapt the holder, years and sites)

Subject: Request for oyster Dermo (Perkinsus marinus) intensity records

I am building an open model of Dermo risk for oyster farms in Maine and am fitting it to published surveillance.
The published summaries give annual means per site; the model needs the oyster-level intensity scores (or
counts per intensity class) behind them, with sampling dates and sample sizes, for [sites] over [years]. Any
format is fine (spreadsheet, database export). The data would be used for model fitting only and cited as
[program name, year of access]; I am glad to share results. If the data are available only under an agreement
or a records request, please tell me the procedure.

## Already obtained without a request
- New Hampshire Fish and Game reports through 2019 (class counts per survey), via the Internet Archive.
- NOAA Mussel Watch per-oyster scores, East Coast, 1988-2010 (public download).
- Ford and Smolowitz 2007 monthly series (digitised from the paper, user-supplied PDF).
- Marquis et al. 2020 Maine monthly qPCR prevalence and copy numbers (digitised from the open-access figure).
